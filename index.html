Security, stated plainly

This demo holds synthetic records only. What you are looking at is the interface; the controls below are what the production deployment implements before a single real policyholder is loaded.

Data
Encrypted, isolated, minimal

TLS in transit, AES-256 at rest, field-level encryption on SSNs, dates of birth and licence numbers. Per-agency partitions, no shared indexes.

Credentials
Never in the browser

Carrier and rater logins live in a server-side vault. The browser receives data, never passwords. The human completes MFA.

Access
Roles and audit

Owner, producer, CSR and read-only. Every view, export, quote and bind is written to an immutable log with user, time and IP.

Compliance
Built for insurance

TCPA consent capture, retention rules, export controls and a deletion path that satisfies state insurance privacy requirements.

Before launch
Tested, not promised

Penetration test and SOC 2 aligned controls before any white-label release. Carrier API or rater agreements before automated pulls.

Honest limits
Portal access is permission

Stored logins are not permission to automate. Some carriers prohibit it, and MFA breaks it. That is why sign-in stays human.
